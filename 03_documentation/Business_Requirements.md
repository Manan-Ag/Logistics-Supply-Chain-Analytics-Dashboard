# Enterprise Supply Chain & Logistics Optimization

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics platform for a global e-commerce enterprise handling 100,000+ annual orders.

The dashboard is designed to help management:
- Identify delivery bottlenecks
- Reduce high shipping costs
- Detect product damage risks
- Monitor warehouse inventory health
- Track supplier reliability

---

# Business Questions

## Executive Financial & Shipping Analytics
- How are shipping costs impacting profitability?
- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
- How are profit margins trending over time?

## Delivery Performance Analytics
- Which delivery partners miss SLA targets most frequently?
- What are the primary causes of shipment delays?
- Which routes experience the highest delivery risk?

## Inventory & Warehouse Analytics
- Which warehouses are approaching stockout risk?
- Which suppliers demonstrate lower reliability?
- How effectively are safety stock thresholds maintained?
